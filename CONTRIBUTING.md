# Contributing

Research Trail is currently an experimental alpha.

Useful contributions include:

- reports from real research use;
- cases where the available entry types or relationships do not fit what actually happened;
- ambiguous distinctions between provenance and epistemic status;
- export/schema problems;
- accessibility and browser-interface issues;
- proposals that improve traceability without turning the tool into a universal compliance checklist.

When reporting a methodological issue, please describe the research situation that produced it. Concrete use cases are more useful at this stage than feature lists.
