# Methodology and Scope

Research Trail is methodological infrastructure for **traceability of an investigation**.

## What the tool can support

The record can make visible:

- the investigation's starting question;
- later questions and conceptual developments;
- explicit assumptions and interpretations;
- provenance of human, AI, source, data, tool, and external contributions;
- hypotheses and predictions;
- analyses, findings, decisions, and claims;
- relationships among these objects;
- contradictions, refinements, rejections, and supersession;
- the route from an eventual claim back through recorded antecedents.

## What the tool does not establish

A complete trail does not establish that:

- the sources are high quality;
- the search was comprehensive;
- the analysis was correct;
- a hypothesis was appropriately specified;
- a claim is true;
- the research complies with a particular disciplinary reporting guideline;
- an AI-generated contribution is reliable.

Those require substantive scientific judgment and, where applicable, separate methods and standards.

## Method-specific overlays

Research Trail is intentionally general. Different investigation types impose different methodological obligations. A systematic review, exploratory conceptual study, empirical experiment, qualitative study, and method-development project should not be audited against one universal checklist.

Future overlays may map a recorded trail to applicable standards or protocols. Such overlays should diagnose visibility/completeness against the chosen standard rather than convert Research Trail into a universal certification system.

## AI provenance

The alpha records AI as one possible origin and joint human–AI interaction as another. This is attribution/provenance metadata only. It does not infer authorship, intellectual responsibility, correctness, or disclosure compliance.

## Evidence retrieval

Search Check is the companion tool for validating literature-search retrieval. Research Trail can reference evidence packages and individual sources, but it should not duplicate database querying, benchmark recovery, relevance sampling, or deduplication.

## Alpha evaluation

The primary question for v0.1 is interactional:

> Can a researcher use the tool during real work without the tool forcing the investigation into an artificial retrospective structure?

Testing should therefore focus first on whether the categories, relationships, and capture flow correspond to real research behavior.
